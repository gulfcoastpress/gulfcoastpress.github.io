Speed to lead for contractors: 5 follow-ups you can automate this weekend

Most service jobs aren't lost on price. They're lost because somebody else called back first. Here are five follow-ups that don't need you to remember anything.

1. Text back missed calls

One reply text, within a minute, to the person who just called. How to build it.

2. Sort urgent leads to the top

A leaking water heater shouldn't sit behind a gutter quote. Have AI label each form lead HOT, WARM or COLD and email you the HOT ones. That one's free here.

3. Answer the 9 PM email the same night

A short, plain reply with your booking link, sent in the same thread. Want to read it first? Save it as a Gmail draft instead.

4. Ask for the review while they're happy

Mark the job complete in your sheet. An hour later the customer gets a thank-you and your review link. Past customers only.

5. Get a Monday summary

One email: how many leads, how many urgent, where they came from, and one thing to fix.
