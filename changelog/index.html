Changelog

What changed

Fixes and updates to the packs and free workflows. Buyers get updated files in their Payhip library.

2 Nov 2026

  • New bonus: Triage Heartbeat (Inbox Operator 08, Core 3 bonus, free on the public gist): once a day it checks that Inbox Triage labelled mail in the last 24 hours and sends a Telegram alert if it went quiet, for example when a Google OAuth app in Testing mode expires its token after 7 days or a self-hosted box goes to sleep. Thanks to the r/n8n commenter who suggested it.

1 Nov 2026

  • Inbox Triage (Inbox Operator 01, free sample, public gist): the trigger skipped mail labelled triaged, but nothing applied that label, so the same email could be sorted again. The workflow now applies a triaged label (LABEL_TRIAGED in Config) after a clean sort. Thanks to the r/n8n commenter who spotted it.
  • Reply Drafter (Inbox Operator 02): same pattern. It now applies a drafted label (LABEL_DRAFTED) so each email gets one draft, and skips failed AI replies instead of saving a blank draft.
  • Lead Catcher (Inbox Operator 04): now applies a logged label (LABEL_LOGGED) so a lead is logged once, and skips failed AI replies.
  • Follow-Up Chaser (Inbox Operator 05): no blank nudge drafts when the AI reply fails; the lead is retried the next morning.
  • Follow-Up Engine 02, 03, 05: failed AI replies no longer create blank drafts or mark a lead as already emailed.
  • Content Engine 03, 04, 06: a source is only marked done when drafts were actually written; failed AI replies are retried the next day. Removed an unused field.
  • Free starter pack: grew from 3 to 5 workflows, each with a README. The RSS to LinkedIn sample no longer queues a row when the AI reply fails.
  • Process: published the QA checklist every release now goes through.

How this is made: the workflows, guides and this site were written with an AI assistant. Before a release, every workflow file goes through our QA checklist: an automated check of connections, Config fields, labels and credentials, plus an import test in n8n 1.123. The store owner reads the changes and approves each release. We do not run every release against live Gmail, Google Sheets or OpenAI accounts, so test with your own email before you switch anything on. Results depend on your setup and use; no income, time-saving or business outcome is guaranteed.
