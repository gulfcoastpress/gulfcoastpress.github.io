Questions before you buy

How long does setup take?

Once, about 15 minutes to connect Gmail, Google Sheets and OpenAI in n8n and create a Sheet and a few Gmail labels. After that, roughly 5 to 15 minutes per workflow: import, fill the Config node, test with an email to yourself, activate. Most buyers start with one or two workflows, not all seven at once.

What does it cost to run?

You need n8n (Cloud plan or the free self-hosted Community Edition) and, for the AI steps, your own OpenAI API key. The AI steps use gpt-4o-mini at OpenAI's published price of $0.15 per 1M input tokens and $0.60 per 1M output tokens (checked 1 Nov 2026; OpenAI can change it). Can I build this myself?

Yes. n8n is open and everything here uses standard nodes. You are paying for the time already spent: the prompts, the JSON parsing, the label bookkeeping that stops repeats, and the edge cases (failed AI replies are retried, not turned into blank drafts). The free starter pack shows the build style, so you can judge before you buy.

Does it send email for me?

No. Anything addressed to a customer is saved as a Gmail draft. The only emails sent automatically are reports to your own address (the morning brief and the weekly pipeline report).

Which n8n version?

n8n 1.x. Each file is import-tested on n8n 1.123.
