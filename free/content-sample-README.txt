RSS to LinkedIn Draft (free sample from The Content Engine)
===========================================================

Each new blog post in your RSS feed becomes one LinkedIn draft in a Google Sheet queue. Nothing is posted.

SET UP (about 10 minutes)
1. Import content-sample.json.
2. Put your feed URL in New Post; make a Sheet with a Queue tab: date, title, url, draft, status; paste the Sheet ID into Config.
3. Connect Google Sheets and OpenAI, test, then activate.

NEEDS: n8n (Cloud trial or free self-hosted) and Google Sheets, an OpenAI API key, an RSS feed you own.
Tested: imports into n8n 1.123.
