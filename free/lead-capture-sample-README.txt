Multi-Source Lead Capture lite (free sample from The Follow-Up Engine)
======================================================================

One webhook for Typeform, Tally, Jotform, scheduler or JSON form leads, normalised and deduped by email into a Google Sheet. No AI.

SET UP (about 10 minutes)
1. Import lead-capture-sample.json.
2. Make a Sheet with a Leads tab: date, name, email, phone, source, need, status, stage. Paste the Sheet ID into Config.
3. Connect Google Sheets, activate, and paste the Production webhook URL into your form tools.

NEEDS: n8n (Cloud trial or free self-hosted) and Google Sheets and a form tool that can send to a webhook.
Tested: imports into n8n 1.123.
